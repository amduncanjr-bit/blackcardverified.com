The group chat has a government and you already know your position

Nobody voted but everybody knows who's in charge.

The group chat is not a casual thing. It is a living institution with structure, precedent, and consequences. People have been removed from group chats and never recovered socially. People have been added to group chats and it changed their entire trajectory. Do not let the blue bubbles fool you into thinking this is informal.

Every group chat has a Founder, and the Founder's power is mostly ceremonial — like the British monarchy, except they can still mute you. They named the chat. They set the tone in the first three messages. After that, real authority migrates to whoever shows up consistently. The Founder gets credit forever but governs very little. This is established.

Then there is the Correspondent. This is the person actually running things. They send the memes before everyone else has seen them. They archive the screenshots. When something happens in the world — anything, a tweet, a trailer, a whole award show — the Correspondent drops it in the chat within four minutes and says "y'all see this" without waiting for an answer. The chat would not survive without them and they will never be properly thanked.

The supporting cast is load-bearing

Below the Correspondent, the roles get more specific. The Reactor never starts a conversation but responds to everything — the laughing emojis, the "NOOO", the single "😭" sent eleven times in a row — and without them the whole enterprise feels like talking into a void. The Lurker reads every message and says nothing for weeks, then surfaces during a crisis with one perfectly placed response that reminds everyone why they were invited in the first place. You need them. Do not kick them out during a purge. That is a mistake people make.

The Thread Ignorer responds to something from four days ago like time is a suggestion. The Voice Memo Person sends four-minute voice notes when a sentence would suffice and everyone listens to every single one. The Devil's Advocate is not always wrong, which is the most annoying thing about them.

The Lurker has read every message since 2019 and will say nothing until it matters, and when it matters, they will say exactly the right thing.

Succession is never discussed and always understood

When a group chat dies — and they do die, quietly, with no funeral — it is usually because the Correspondent got busy or got tired. The chat limps along for a few weeks on old energy, someone sends a meme that gets one reaction, and then nothing. Forty-seven unread messages, all of them silence. A successor could step up. They rarely do. The Correspondent role cannot be assigned; it has to be assumed with full confidence or the chat can smell the hesitation.

New chats form the same way new nations form: a splinter group with a grievance, a specific purpose, or a vibe that the main chat could not hold. The Couples Chat. The Work Friends Who Are Actually Friends Chat. The Chat That Was Made For One Event And Is Now Just Your Real Friends. These are not replacements. They are parallel governments, each with their own constitution, their own norms, their own rules about what gets screenshotted and what stays in the room.

You already know which chat you actually check first. You already know which notification you ignore. You already know what role you play — and if you don't, ask yourself who the chat would feel emptier without. That's your answer, and you already knew it before you finished reading this sentence.
